Clinical End-to-End Workflow & Safeguards
1. Finding the Right Spot (Canal Cropping)

Targeting levels L1/L2 through L5/S1. Mid-sagittal slices are targeted where the thecal sac is widest, and anatomical bounding crops isolate the spinal canal from subcutaneous fat and noise.

2. No Cheating (Patient Splitting)

All slices and levels belonging to a patient (study_id) are grouped into strictly one partition using GroupShuffleSplit. Completely eliminates test patient leakage.

3. Class Imbalance Handling

Severe cases comprise ~10% of real scans. The pipeline implements inverse-frequency class weights and Multi-Class Focal Loss (γ=2.0) to prioritize hard severe stenosis cases.

4. Grad-CAM Visual Proof

Computes gradient importance on final spatial feature representations to generate color-coded heatmaps over MRI slices, verifying the AI looks at canal geometry rather than background.
